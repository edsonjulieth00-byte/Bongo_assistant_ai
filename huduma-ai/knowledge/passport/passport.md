## Passport types

The Immigration Department passport guide identifies:
- Ordinary Passport.
- Service Passport.
- Diplomatic Passport.

The ordinary passport is issued to Tanzanian citizens for travel outside Tanzania. The guide states that the ordinary passport has a validity of ten years.

## Basic documents for an ordinary passport application

The official e-Immigration passport guideline lists:
1. Applicant's birth certificate.
2. Parent's birth certificate or affidavit of birth.
3. National Identification Card (NIDA).
4. Recent passport photograph with a light-blue background.
5. Form fee of TZS 20,000.
6. Evidence of travel or evidence of the applicant's activity.

The Tanzania Government portal also lists birth/citizenship evidence for the applicant, parent citizenship/birth evidence, NIDA and a passport-size photo.

## Online application process

The official passport portal allows an applicant to:
1. Start a new application.
2. Complete the application information accurately and fully.
3. Save the application/reference number for future use.
4. Enter previous passport information if applicable.
5. Enter guarantor information and emergency-contact information where requested.
6. Attach required supporting documents.
7. Review the information.
8. Accept the declaration if the information is correct.
9. Pay after receiving the payment control number.
10. Use the status function to follow the application.

The portal provides options to continue an incomplete application using the Application Number and Reference ID.

## Passport replacement

The government portal states that a passport may be replaced when it has expired or when its visa pages are full.

The passport guide also contains procedures for lost, stolen and damaged passports. For a lost or stolen passport, the guide states that the loss should be reported to the police and Immigration authorities, and additional evidence/procedures may be required.

For a damaged passport, the guide states that the applicant should submit a new online application, an application letter explaining the circumstances and the damaged passport.

## Fees

The official government/Immigration sources currently state:
- Passport application/form fee: TZS 20,000 at the relevant application stage.
- Ordinary passport fee: TZS 150,000 in Tanzania.
- Emergency Travel Document: TZS 20,000 in Tanzania.

The Immigration passport guide also contains separate fees for lost and damaged passports. The chatbot should quote those only from the current official fee guidance in the knowledge base.

## Emergency Travel Document

The official passport guide states that an Emergency Travel Document is for a Tanzanian citizen with an urgent trip who has not completed the ordinary passport process, cannot obtain a passport in time, or is stranded abroad/being returned to Tanzania.

The document is intended for a single trip to the relevant country and return to Tanzania.

## Processing times

The Immigration Client Service Charter states service standards for passport issuance, including different periods depending on where the application is submitted. Because these standards may be revised, the chatbot should prefer the latest official Immigration source available in the knowledge base.

## Important chatbot rule

Do not state a passport fee, processing time or required document as universal if the official source indicates that it depends on application type, location or applicant circumstances.
