# Lost or Stolen Tanzania Passport

A lost or stolen Tanzanian passport should be reported immediately.

The official passport guide instructs passport holders to report a lost passport to an Immigration Office, Police Station, the nearest Embassy of the United Republic of Tanzania, or an Embassy of an East African Community member state.

The passport guide contains separate charges for repeated loss. For a passport lost for the second time, the guide states:
- Ordinary passport: TZS 750,000 or USD 365.
- Service passport: TZS 750,000 or USD 365.
- Diplomatic passport: TZS 750,000 or USD 365.

Applicants should follow the current Immigration instructions for the replacement process and any required loss report or supporting documents.
