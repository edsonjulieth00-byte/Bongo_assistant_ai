# Tanzania Passport Fees

The Tanzania Immigration e-Services portal requires payment after an applicant receives the relevant payment Control Number.

The current official passport and travel-document information includes:
- Passport application/form fee: TZS 20,000.
- Ordinary passport: TZS 150,000.
- Emergency Travel Document: TZS 20,000 in Tanzania and USD 20 abroad.

The official passport guide also lists additional fees for lost and damaged passports. For a damaged passport, the guide states TZS 300,000 for an ordinary, service or diplomatic passport.

Applicants should use the current official Immigration payment instructions and Control Number because fees and payment arrangements can be updated.
