# Types of Tanzanian Passports

Tanzania issues three main passport types:
1. Ordinary Passport.
2. Service Passport.
3. Diplomatic Passport.

An Ordinary Passport is issued to Tanzanian citizens for ordinary international travel.

A Service Passport is issued to eligible persons who qualify for a service passport under the applicable Government rules.

A Diplomatic Passport is issued to eligible persons who qualify under the applicable diplomatic passport rules.

The specific eligibility and supporting documents depend on the passport type and the applicant's status.
