# Damaged Tanzania Passport

A damaged Tanzanian passport should be reported to the Immigration authorities.

The official passport guide provides a separate fee category for a damaged passport. The guide states a fee of:
- Ordinary passport: TZS 300,000.
- Service passport: TZS 300,000.
- Diplomatic passport: TZS 300,000.

The official guide also warns that a passport is Government property and should not be altered or handled by an unauthorised person.

Applicants should report a damaged passport promptly to the nearest Immigration Office, and follow the current instructions given by Immigration for replacement.
