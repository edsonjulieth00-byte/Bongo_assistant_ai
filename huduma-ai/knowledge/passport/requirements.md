# Tanzania Passport Application Requirements

The current Tanzania Immigration e-Services passport guideline lists the following documents and information for a passport application:

1. Applicant's birth certificate.
2. Parent's birth certificate or affidavit of birth.
3. National Identification card (NIDA).
4. Recent passport photograph with a light-blue background.
5. Passport application/form fee of TZS 20,000.
6. Evidence of travel or evidence of the activity undertaken by the applicant.

During the online application, the applicant is also required to:
- Provide accurate and complete personal information.
- Provide previous passport information if the applicant has previously held a passport.
- Provide guarantor information.
- Provide emergency-contact information.
- Attach the required supporting documents.
- Review and confirm the application before payment.
- Pay after receiving a payment Control Number.

The online portal instructs applicants to print the completed application and submit it together with the required supporting documents to the appropriate Immigration Office for processing.

Applicants should use the current official Immigration guideline because document requirements may change.
