# NIDA National Identification Number (NIN)

NIN means National Identification Number issued through NIDA.

NIDA provides an online service for citizens to find their NIN.

The NIN lookup service requires:
- First name.
- Surname.
- Date of birth in DD-MM-YYYY format.
- Mother's first name.
- Mother's surname.
- The displayed verification text.

The information should be entered accurately.

NIDA's online self-service portal also provides services for finding a NIN, generating payment control numbers for replacement National IDs, and printing bills.

NIN lookup is separate from applying for a new National ID. If a user needs to correct registration information or replace an ID, the relevant NIDA procedure should be followed.
