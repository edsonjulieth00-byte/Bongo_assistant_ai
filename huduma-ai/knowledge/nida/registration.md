# NIDA Registration for Tanzanian Citizens

NIDA provides two ways for a Tanzanian citizen to begin registration:
1. Online registration.
2. Normal registration through a NIDA registration office.

Citizen registration requires documents establishing identity, age, citizenship and residence as required by NIDA.

Key citizen application attachments include:
- Birth certificate.
- Residence identification letter from the relevant local government authority.
- Birth certificate or National ID of one parent.

Applicants should complete information accurately and ensure that information entered agrees with supporting documents.

NIDA's online registration guidance advises applicants to provide accurate residence details, including house number, street and ward where applicable.

Where an applicant has parents who already have NIDA NINs, the relevant supporting documents should be provided as required by the online registration guidance.

Applicants who have school certificates, TIN or voter identification may attach them where available.

After online registration, the applicant may need to print the completed information, obtain the required local government stamp and signature, and attend the NIDA district office with hard copies of uploaded supporting documents to complete registration.

Registration includes verification and biometric capture such as fingerprints, photograph and electronic signature.

Applicants should bring original documents when attending registration or biometric capture where NIDA requires them.
