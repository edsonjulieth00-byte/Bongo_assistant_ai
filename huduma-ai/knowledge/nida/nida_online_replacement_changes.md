## NIDA online services

NIDA's online portal lists services including:
- Self Service for citizens to request their NIN.
- Generation of government payment control numbers for ID replacement.
- Printing bills.
- Verification services for NIDA stakeholders.

## Finding a NIN

The NIDA online NIN service asks the user to enter identifying information and a CAPTCHA. The service is intended to help a person find their NIN.

## Replacing a lost citizen National ID

NIDA states that a citizen who loses the National ID should:

1. Obtain a police loss/property report.
2. Present the police loss report at the NIDA district registration office where registered or another nearby NIDA registration office.
3. Obtain a government payment control number.
4. Pay the applicable replacement contribution.
5. Submit the payment receipt to the NIDA registration office to complete the remaining replacement procedure.

NIDA currently states the replacement contribution for a citizen as:
- First replacement: TZS 20,000.
- Second replacement: TZS 30,000.
- Third or subsequent replacement: TZS 50,000.

## Replacing a damaged citizen National ID

NIDA states that the applicant should:
1. Present the damaged National ID at the NIDA registration office.
2. Obtain a government payment control number.
3. Pay the applicable contribution.
4. Submit the payment receipt to complete the remaining procedure.

The stated contribution is:
- First replacement: TZS 20,000.
- Second replacement: TZS 30,000.
- Third or subsequent replacement: TZS 50,000.

## Changing registration information

NIDA provides guidance for correction/change of registration information, including:
- Names.
- Date/month of birth.
- Residence information.
- Certain public-service employee records.
- Other categories specified by NIDA.

For name changes, NIDA states that supporting evidence may include documents such as birth records, marriage or divorce documents, school certificates, baptismal records, deed poll documentation, government gazette publication and other evidence requested by the district registration officer, depending on the case.

NIDA states that changes without appropriate documentary evidence cannot be accepted or processed.

For changes where the error was made by NIDA personnel, NIDA states that the correction may proceed without the applicant paying the normal correction charge, subject to verification.

## Resident foreigners and refugees

NIDA has separate procedures for changes involving resident foreigners and refugees. Requirements can include passport, valid residence documentation, local-government letters, dependency documents and other documents depending on the person's category.
