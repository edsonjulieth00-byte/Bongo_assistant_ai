# Replacement of a Lost or Damaged NIDA National ID

A Tanzanian citizen whose National ID has been lost or damaged can apply for a replacement through NIDA.

For a lost National ID:
1. Obtain a police loss report.
2. Submit the report to a NIDA district registration office where the applicant was registered or another nearby NIDA registration office.
3. Obtain a Government payment control number.
4. Pay the applicable replacement contribution.
5. Submit the payment receipt to NIDA to complete the remaining procedures.

For a damaged National ID:
1. Submit the damaged National ID to a NIDA district registration office where the applicant was registered or another nearby NIDA registration office.
2. Obtain a Government payment control number.
3. Pay the applicable replacement contribution.
4. Submit the payment receipt to NIDA to complete the remaining procedures.

Current replacement costs stated by NIDA are:
- First replacement: TZS 20,000.
- Second replacement: TZS 30,000.
- Third and subsequent replacements: TZS 50,000.

NIDA online services can be used to request a payment control number for replacement services and to print a bill.

Applicants should follow instructions given by NIDA registration officers for remaining steps.
