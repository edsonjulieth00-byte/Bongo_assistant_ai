# NIDA — Registration and Identification of Tanzanian Citizens

Source authority: National Identification Authority (NIDA), Tanzania
Official sources:
- https://nida.go.tz/Usajili-Raia
- https://eonline.nida.go.tz/
- https://services.nida.go.tz/
- https://services.nida.go.tz/get_nin

## Citizen registration

NIDA states that Tanzanian citizens can register through online registration or the normal registration process.

For the citizen application process, NIDA states that the applicant should:

1. Complete National Identification application form No. 1A in black ink and capital letters.
2. Sign the application in the relevant signature section, or provide a thumbprint where applicable.
3. Have the application form stamped by the local government authority where the applicant lives and obtain a residence identification letter.
4. Attach the required supporting documents.

## Required documents stated by NIDA for citizens

NIDA lists these as required attachments for citizen registration:

1. Applicant's birth certificate.
2. Residence identification letter from the local government where the applicant lives.
3. Birth certificate or National ID of one parent.

NIDA's online registration service also states that citizens should have:
- Applicant birth certificate.
- Copy of a parent's birth certificate or affidavit.
- Where citizenship is by descent, evidence such as a parent's birth certificate or National ID.
- Where citizenship is by registration, the applicant's registration citizenship dossier number.

## Online registration

NIDA's online service allows an applicant to start registration electronically and then print the application and submit it together with supporting documents to a NIDA district office for biometric registration.

The online service states that an applicant should have:
- An email address used for registration and receiving a completion link.
- At least two identity documents for verification.
- Scanned supporting documents in PDF format, or JPG/PNG images, subject to the online system's stated file requirements.

## Biometric registration

NIDA describes the registration process as including:
- Review of applicant information.
- Capture of fingerprints.
- Photograph capture.
- Electronic signature.
- Final verification.
- Printing, quality verification and issuance of the National ID.

## NIN

NIDA provides an online service for finding the National Identification Number (NIN). The service asks for identifying information including first name, surname, date of birth, mother's first name and mother's surname.
