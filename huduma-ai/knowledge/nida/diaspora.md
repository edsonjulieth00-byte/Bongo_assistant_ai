# NIDA Registration for Tanzanian Diaspora

NIDA provides registration and identification services for Tanzanian citizens living outside Tanzania.

For diaspora registration, the applicant should complete the NIDA National Identification application form and obtain residence identification from a Tanzanian Embassy in the country of residence. Where Tanzania has no embassy in that country, the residence letter may be obtained from a Tanzanian embassy in a neighbouring country.

Required attachments stated by NIDA include:
- Birth certificate.
- Residence identification letter from a Tanzanian embassy.
- Birth certificate or National ID of one parent.

Additional documents that may be attached, if available, include education certificates, passport, driving licence, TIN, health insurance card and Zanzibar resident identification card.

The applicant submits the application to the embassy that issued the residence identification letter for biometric registration, including photograph, electronic signature and fingerprints.

Applicants should bring original documents that can prove citizenship and age when attending biometric registration.

For online registration, Tanzanians living outside Tanzania should obtain confirmation of residence in the country of residence from a Tanzanian embassy so that residence information can be entered correctly.

The availability of a specific diaspora registration exercise or location can change, so applicants should confirm current arrangements with NIDA or the relevant Tanzanian embassy.
