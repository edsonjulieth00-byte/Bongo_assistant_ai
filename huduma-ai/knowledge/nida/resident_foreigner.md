# NIDA Registration for Resident Foreigners

Resident foreigners aged 18 or above who live in Tanzania according to a valid residence document are required to register with NIDA at a NIDA district registration office.

A resident foreigner should present:
1. Passport from the country of origin.
2. Valid work permit.
3. Residence permit, in two copies.
4. Bank payment slip according to the applicant's category after obtaining the required Government payment control number.

NIDA's published categories and fees include:
- Investor: USD 100.
- Employee: USD 50.
- Missionary, researcher or student: USD 20.
- Dependant: USD 20.

Payments are made through the NIDA bank account at NMB, CRDB, NBC or PBZ after obtaining the relevant control number from the NIDA district registration office or applicable electronic service.

Applicants should follow NIDA registration-office instructions regarding any additional documents or steps required for their individual case.
