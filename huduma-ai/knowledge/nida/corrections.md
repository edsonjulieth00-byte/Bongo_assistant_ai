# NIDA Registration Information Corrections

NIDA allows eligible applicants to request corrections to registration information through a NIDA district registration office.

Eligible situations include marriage-related name changes, legal divorce-related restoration of previous names, errors made by the applicant during registration, and information entered incorrectly in the NIDA system because of an operational issue.

Information that may be corrected, subject to NIDA requirements and supporting evidence, includes first, middle or surname; date and month of birth; residence; telephone number; citizenship where supported by acceptable documents; marital information; employment information; and specified personal information.

The following information is not normally changed unless special approval is granted after review by the Registration and Identification Committee and approval by the Director General:
- Parents' information.
- Permanent residence.
- Place of birth.
- Two or three names.
- Year of birth.
- Applicant's signature.

NIDA may require additional documents to prove the requested information. A request without appropriate supporting evidence may not be accepted or processed.

For name changes, the district registration officer may request additional documents proving the applicant's names or other relevant information.

A correction fee may apply. NIDA's current name-change guidance states TZS 20,000 for changing the relevant information. If the error was caused by NIDA, the registration officer may proceed without requiring the applicant to pay the correction cost.

A correction request may be approved or rejected depending on the reason and supporting evidence.
