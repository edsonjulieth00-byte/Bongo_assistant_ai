# Types of Tanzania Visa

Tanzania Immigration Services issues six main types of visa:

1. Ordinary Visa (Single Entry)
2. Multiple Entry Visa
3. Business Visa
4. Transit Visa
5. Student Visa
6. Gratis Visa

## Ordinary Visa

The Ordinary Visa is commonly used for tourism and other permitted short visits such as visiting friends or relatives, attending meetings, workshops or exhibitions. It is valid for up to 90 days. Ordinary Visa holders are not permitted to work or conduct business under this visa.

United States passport holders travelling for tourism are required to apply for a Multiple Entry Visa rather than an Ordinary Visa.

## Multiple Entry Visa

A Multiple Entry Visa may be issued for purposes including tourism, visiting friends or relatives, or attending conferences. It may have a maximum validity of 12 months, but the maximum consecutive stay is three months.

## Business Visa

A Business Visa may be issued for short-term lawful business-related activities, including special assignments, short-term professional work, auditing, feasibility studies, establishing business contacts and arrangements for investments during a company's start-up period. It is limited to a maximum of 90 days and is single-entry. It should not be used instead of a Residence Permit when the intended activity exceeds three months.

## Transit Visa

A Transit Visa is for foreign nationals entering Tanzania while travelling onward to another country. It is issued for a period not exceeding seven days. It is not intended for tourism or other purposes.

## Gratis Visa

A Gratis Visa may be issued to holders of diplomatic, service or official passports or recognised international-organisation travel documents who enter Tanzania for official purposes, together with eligible dependants. It may also be issued in other cases as directed by the Commissioner General of Immigration or Commissioner of Immigration Zanzibar.

## Student Visa

A Student Visa may be issued to foreign nationals entering Tanzania for academic purposes such as research, internship, volunteering, exchange programmes or study at registered institutions. The applicable validity and fee depend on the programme and duration.

Applicants should choose the visa type that matches their actual purpose of travel.
