# Tanzania Visa Requirements

General requirements for a Tanzania visa application include:
- A valid passport with at least six months validity.
- At least one unused visa page in the passport.
- A completed online visa application and declaration.
- A recent passport-size photograph meeting the official photo specifications.
- A copy of the passport biographic-data page.
- A return ticket where required by the visa category and official guidance.
- Payment of the applicable visa fee.
- Additional supporting documents required for the particular visa type or category.

For an online visa photograph, the official guidance states that the photograph should be coloured, show the applicant's full face facing the camera, have a plain background, and meet the stated file and size limits.

The exact supporting documents depend on the visa type and purpose of travel.

Ordinary Visa applications require a passport copy, return ticket, completed application and declaration, and the applicable visa fee.

Business Visa applications require a passport copy, return ticket, the USD 250 fee and evidence of the work or assignment to be performed for a period not exceeding three months.

Transit Visa applicants may be required to provide evidence of a visa for the destination country and/or an onward ticket.

Student Visa applicants must provide the required passport copy, applicable fee, a covering letter from the host institution describing the course or field and duration, and the required return-ticket documentation.

Applicants should upload all supporting documents required for the selected visa category.
