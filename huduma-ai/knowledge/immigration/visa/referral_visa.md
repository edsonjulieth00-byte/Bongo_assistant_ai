# Tanzania Referral Visa

Some nationalities and applicants fall under Tanzania's Referral Visa category. The list of countries and nationals subject to referral requirements may be reviewed by Immigration from time to time.

Applicants under the Referral Visa category:
1. Must submit their visa applications online and wait for approval before travelling, unless they have obtained the required approval from the Office of the Commissioner General of Immigration.
2. Are advised to submit applications at least two months before the intended travel date.
3. Should provide complete local host information, including physical address and contact details where available.
4. Should upload an invitation letter or visa application letter addressed to the Commissioner General of Immigration when required.

Applicants in the Referral Visa category are not eligible to use visa on arrival unless they have obtained the required approval from the Commissioner General of Immigration.

Referral applicants are advised not to pay for flight tickets, accommodation or other reservations before their visa has been approved. When applying online, they may use a trip itinerary instead of a flight ticket where the official guidance allows this.

Referral visa processing may take longer than ordinary online visa processing.

The list of referral countries can change. Applicants should check the current official Tanzania Immigration visa information before applying.
