# Tanzania Visa Fees

Current visa fees listed by the Tanzania Immigration visa guidelines are:

- Ordinary Visa: USD 50.
- Multiple Entry Visa: USD 100.
- Business Visa: USD 250.
- Transit Visa: USD 30.
- Student Visa: the applicable fee depends on the student category and duration; the official guidelines list USD 50, USD 250, USD 300 and USD 550 for different categories.
- Gratis Visa: no visa fee.

The Ordinary Visa may be valid for up to 90 days.

A Multiple Entry Visa may have a maximum validity of 12 months, but the holder may stay for a maximum of three consecutive months on a visit.

A Business Visa has a maximum validity of 90 days and is issued on a single-entry basis.

A Transit Visa is issued for a period not exceeding seven days.

Visa fees are paid online through the official visa application system. Visa fees are pre-paid and non-refundable under the visa terms and conditions.

Applicants should check the official visa portal before payment because fees and conditions may change.
