# Tanzania Visa — Official Guidelines

Source authority: Tanzania Immigration Services Department
Official sources:
- https://visa.immigration.go.tz/guidelines
- https://visa.immigration.go.tz/start
- https://visa.immigration.go.tz/termsandconditions
- https://www.immigration.go.tz/index.php/regulations

## What a visa is

The official Tanzania Visa Guidelines define a visa as an authorization given to a foreigner to travel and present themselves for entry clearance into Tanzania.

A Visa Grant Notice informs an applicant of authorization to enter subject to fulfillment of other entry conditions at the point of entry.

## How to apply

The official guidelines state that Tanzania has two visa options:
1. Apply online through the official Tanzania Immigration Services website.
2. Obtain a visa on arrival at an official entry point.

The guidelines recommend using the online visa application platform.

Applicants are advised to use official Tanzania Immigration channels only.

## Passport requirements

The visa terms state that applicants should have a passport with:
- At least six months validity.
- At least one unused visa page.

Applicants must select the correct visa type and category. The official terms warn that selecting the wrong visa type can lead to rejection.

Supporting documents required for the selected visa type must be uploaded as instructed.

## Visa types

The current official guidelines identify six visa categories:
- Ordinary (single-entry) visa.
- Multiple-entry visa.
- Business visa.
- Transit visa.
- Student visa.
- Gratis visa (diplomatic).

## Stated visa fees

The official guidelines currently list:
- Ordinary visa: USD 50.
- Multiple-entry visa: USD 100.
- Business visa: USD 250.
- Transit visa: USD 30.
- Student visa: category-dependent fees stated by the official guideline.
- Gratis visa: no fee for the applicable diplomatic category.

## Ordinary visa

The guidelines state that ordinary visas are used for tourism and certain other permitted purposes such as meetings, visiting friends or relatives, workshops and exhibitions.

Holders of an ordinary visa are not authorized to work or conduct business under that visa.

## Important travel condition

The official guidelines state that visitors must show a return ticket upon arrival at the entry point in Tanzania.

## Referral and special cases

The official guidelines contain special rules for applicants whose nationality/status falls under referral visa procedures, as well as refugees and stateless persons. These cases should be answered using the detailed current official guideline rather than general assumptions.

## Important chatbot rule

Visa eligibility can depend on nationality, travel purpose, passport type and other circumstances. The chatbot must not tell a user that a visa is guaranteed based only on nationality or purpose unless the official knowledge explicitly supports that conclusion.
