# Residence Permit Class B

Residence Permit Class B is issued to foreigners who possess qualifications or skills, preferably in highly technical occupations, that are not readily available in the local labour market.

It covers specific employment by a specific employer and professionals working in government or private strategic projects.

Class B applications must normally be made before the prospective employee enters Tanzania.

General Class B requirements include:

1. Duly completed online application.
2. Application letter.
3. Work Permit issued by the Labour Commissioner.
4. Passport with at least six months validity.
5. Employer covering letter.
6. Passport-size photograph.
7. Full-size photograph.
8. Employee CV.
9. Certified academic certificates.
10. Official translation where documents or certificates are written in languages other than English or Swahili.
11. Job description.
12. Employment contract.
13. Previous original Class B permit for renewal or replacement.
14. Organization structure of the company.
15. Professional registration certificates where required.
16. Business licence.
17. Memorandum and Articles of Association or relevant Registrar extract.
18. Certificate of incorporation, certificate of compliance, business name registration or certificate of change of name.
19. Letter of no objection where the applicant has shifted from another company.
20. Tax clearance.
21. TIN certificate.

The current official Class B matrix includes:

B-1 — Specific employment by a specific employer: USD 2,000 for non-EAC applicants and USD 500 for EAC citizens.

B-2 — Specific employment by a specific employer for a Tanzanian Diaspora: USD 500.

B-3 — Specific employment by a specific employer for a period not exceeding six months: USD 500 for non-EAC applicants, USD 250 for EAC citizens and USD 250 for Diaspora applicants.

B-4 — Specific employment by a specific employer for refugees: USD 500.

Professional applicants may also need registration or clearance from the relevant professional body, depending on the occupation.
