# Residence Permit Class A

Residence Permit Class A is for foreigners intending to engage in self-employed investment in Tanzania.

The official guidelines cover investment activities including mining and prospecting, agriculture and animal husbandry, professional and consultancy services, professional arts, manufacturing and processing, larger-scale investment and trade, hospitality, information technology, transport, construction, real estate and other investments.

Class A applications may be submitted electronically through Immigration Headquarters, the Zanzibar Immigration Head Office, Tanzania Investment Centre (TIC), Export Processing Zones Authority (EPZA) and Zanzibar Investment Promotion Authority (ZIPA), as applicable.

General Class A requirements include:

1. Duly completed online application.
2. Application letter.
3. Work Permit issued by the Labour Commissioner.
4. Passport with at least six months validity.
5. Passport-size photograph.
6. Full-size photograph.
7. Registration certificate from the relevant authority where the business requires sector authorization.
8. Certificate of Incentive where applicable for projects registered with TIC, ZIPA or EPZA.
9. Board resolution where applicable.
10. Memorandum and Articles of Association or relevant Registrar extract.
11. Certificate of incorporation, certificate of compliance, business name registration or certificate of change of name.
12. Valid business licence.
13. TIN certificate.
14. Valid tax clearance certificate.
15. Tax payment slips where applicable.
16. Lease agreement or Certificate of Occupancy.
17. Company asset list.
18. Physical Verification Visit Report where necessary.
19. Company current bank statement where necessary.

The official Class A matrix includes different categories and fees. Examples include A-1 Professional Artist Investor, A-2 Diaspora Investors, A-3 Other Sector/Industry Investors and A-4 Refugee Investors.

The current official guideline lists A-1 at USD 1,000 for non-EAC applicants and USD 500 for EAC citizens; A-2 Diaspora at USD 1,000; A-3 at USD 3,000 for non-EAC applicants and USD 1,500 for EAC citizens; and A-4 at USD 1,500.

The applicable fee depends on the permit category and applicant status. Applicants should use the current official matrix when applying.
