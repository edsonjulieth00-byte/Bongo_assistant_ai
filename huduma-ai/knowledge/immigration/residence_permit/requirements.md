# Residence Permit — General Requirements

General requirements depend on the residence permit class and the applicant's circumstances.

The official guidelines state that applicants should provide the documents required for their particular class and category, and that documents attached to an online application should be certified.

General requirements and conditions include:

1. A duly completed online application.
2. An application or covering letter where required.
3. A valid passport, generally with at least six months validity.
4. Passport-size photograph.
5. A full-size photograph where required.
6. Work Permit issued by the Labour Commissioner for applicants who are required to have one.
7. Relevant registration certificates or professional licences where the activity requires authorization.
8. Supporting documents specific to the residence permit class and category.
9. Official translations for documents or certificates written in languages other than English or Swahili where required.

Class A applicants may additionally need business and investment documents such as company registration documents, business licence, TIN, tax clearance, tax payment evidence, lease or occupancy documents, company asset information and other sector-specific documents.

Class B applicants may additionally need an employer covering letter, CV, academic certificates, job description, employment contract, organization structure and professional registration where applicable.

Class C applicants may need documents relating to the particular purpose, such as a research permit, retirement evidence, volunteer contract, missionary documents, medical evidence, court-case evidence, internship approval or institutional documents.

The exact documents vary by class, category, occupation and application status. Applicants should use the current official Immigration guidelines for the applicable category.
