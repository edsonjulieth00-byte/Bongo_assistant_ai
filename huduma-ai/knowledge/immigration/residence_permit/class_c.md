# Residence Permit Class C

Residence Permit Class C is for foreigners who are not eligible for Class A or Class B.

The official categories include researchers, retired persons, missionaries, people winding up affairs after employment, volunteers, people receiving medical treatment, NGO employees, interns, professional artists, real-estate buyers in the specified category, refugees and other eligible persons.

Applications should normally be made before entry into Tanzania, except for retired persons and people winding up affairs as stated in the official guidelines.

General Class C requirements include:

1. Duly completed online application.
2. Covering letter from the relevant institution, school or mission.
3. Work Permit or applicable exemption letter.
4. Passport with at least six months validity.
5. Passport-size photograph.
6. Full-size photograph.
7. Relevant registration certificates where required.
8. Applicant CV.
9. Job description where applicable.
10. Certified academic certificates where applicable.
11. Official translations where required.
12. Certificate of registration of the institution, school or mission where applicable.
13. Relevant constitution pages of an organization where applicable.
14. Previous original Class C permit for renewal or replacement.
15. Letter of no objection where a permit holder changes to a new institution.

Examples of additional documents include:

Researchers — research permit from COSTECH, CV, academic certificates and an introduction letter from the relevant university, college or institute.

Volunteers — volunteer contract, job description, evidence of NGO activities, organization constitution and Physical Verification Visit Report where necessary.

Missionaries — relevant job description, professional registration where applicable, ordination certificate for priests, volunteer contract for missionary volunteers and relevant professional licences.

Retired persons — evidence of retirement benefits, surrender of previous residence permit, retirement letter and evidence of place of occupancy.

Medical treatment — evidence of sickness from a government medical hospital and evidence concerning the host.

Court cases — evidence of the trial, passport copy and application forms.

Interns — host institution approval, evidence of sponsorship or scholarship, and relevant education authority approval where applicable.
