# Residence Permit — Fees

Residence Permit fees depend on the class, permit category and applicant status.

The current official Immigration guideline lists the following principal fees.

## Class A

A-1 Professional Artist Investor:
- USD 1,000 for non-EAC applicants.
- USD 500 for EAC citizens.

A-2 Diaspora Investor:
- USD 1,000 for Diaspora applicants.

A-3 Other Sector/Industry Investor:
- USD 3,000 for non-EAC applicants.
- USD 1,500 for EAC citizens.

A-4 Refugee Investor:
- USD 1,500.

## Class B

B-1 Specific employment by specific employer:
- USD 2,000 for non-EAC applicants.
- USD 500 for EAC citizens.

B-2 Specific employment by specific employer — Diaspora:
- USD 500.

B-3 Specific employment by specific employer for a period not exceeding six months:
- USD 500 for non-EAC applicants.
- USD 250 for EAC citizens.
- USD 250 for Diaspora applicants.

B-4 Specific employment by specific employer — Refugees:
- USD 500.

## Class C

C-1 Researchers:
- USD 500 non-EAC.
- USD 250 EAC.
- USD 250 Diaspora.

C-2 Retired persons:
- USD 500 non-EAC.
- USD 250 EAC.
- USD 250 Diaspora.

C-3 Approved religious activities:
- USD 200 non-EAC.
- USD 100 EAC.
- USD 100 Diaspora.

C-4 Winding up affairs:
- USD 200 non-EAC.
- USD 100 EAC.
- USD 100 Diaspora.

C-5 Volunteers:
- USD 200 non-EAC.
- USD 100 EAC.
- USD 100 Diaspora.

C-6 Medical treatment:
- USD 200 non-EAC.
- USD 100 EAC.
- USD 100 Diaspora.

C-7 NGO employees:
- USD 500 non-EAC.
- USD 250 EAC.
- USD 250 Diaspora.

C-8 Parties and witnesses in courts and tribunals:
- USD 500 non-EAC.
- USD 250 EAC.
- USD 250 Diaspora.

C-9 Interns:
- USD 200 non-EAC.
- USD 100 EAC.
- USD 100 Diaspora.

C-10 Professional Artists:
- USD 500 non-EAC.
- USD 250 EAC.
- USD 250 Diaspora.

C-11 Buyer of a real estate:
- USD 500 non-EAC.
- USD 250 EAC.
- USD 250 Diaspora.

C-12 Refugees:
- No fee listed in the current matrix.

C-13 Others:
- USD 500 non-EAC.
- USD 250 EAC.
- USD 250 Diaspora.

Fees can change. The chatbot should use the current official Immigration fee matrix when answering fee questions.
