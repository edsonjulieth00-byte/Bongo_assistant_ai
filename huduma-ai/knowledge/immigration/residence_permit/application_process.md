# Residence Permit — Application Process

The official e-Residence Permit application process is:

1. Visit the Tanzania Immigration e-Residence Permit portal.
2. Read the Residence Permit Guidelines before starting.
3. Create an account using the required contact and account information.
4. Complete the online residence permit application.
5. Attach the required documents. The official guidelines state that documents should be certified.
6. Submit the application.
7. If the application is approved for payment, the applicant is notified.
8. Pay the prescribed fee within 60 days of approval for payment. If payment is not made within 60 days, the application is automatically deleted.
9. After payment, the applicant receives a Residence Permit Enrolment Notification.
10. Report to the nearest Immigration Office or the designated office for fingerprints and photograph capture.
11. After enrolment, the permit proceeds to processing and issuance.
12. The permit may be collected at the applicable Immigration office or designated location.

If the application has insufficient documents or information, Immigration may send an online query through the Residence Permit System. The applicant must rectify the relevant issue so the application can proceed.

Applicants should keep their application information and monitor the application through the official system.
