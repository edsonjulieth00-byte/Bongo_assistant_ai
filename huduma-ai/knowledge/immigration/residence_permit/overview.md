# Tanzania Residence Permits — Overview

A Residence Permit may be issued to a foreigner intending to reside in the United Republic of Tanzania for investment, business, employment, or another legal activity.

There are three main classes:

1. Class A — for self-employed foreigners and investors.
2. Class B — for expatriates employed by companies, private institutions, government or strategic projects where the required skills are not readily available in the local labour market.
3. Class C — for other eligible foreigners, including researchers, retired persons, missionaries, volunteers, interns, people receiving medical treatment, and people attending court cases.

Foreigners intending to reside in Tanzania for more than 90 days for business, work, investment or another acceptable purpose are generally required to hold a residence permit.

Applications are made through the Tanzania Immigration e-Residence Permit system. Applicants should read the applicable guidelines, create an account, complete the online application, attach certified documents and submit the application.

For Class B and Class C, the applicant should normally obtain the residence permit before entering Tanzania. Class A has different treatment under the official guidelines.

Most applicants must first obtain a Work Permit from the Labour Commissioner. Exceptions include purposes such as studies, research, attending court cases and retirement.

Residence Permit issuance statuses include First Grant (FG), Returnee (RT), Second Grant (SG), Replacement (RPL), Re-Grant 1 (RG1) and Re-Grant 2 (RG2).

The Commissioner General of Immigration Services has the authority to issue residence permits.
