# Landing Pass

A Landing Pass is an Immigration pass associated with permission to land and remain in Tanzania under the conditions applicable to the particular traveller.

The pass is subject to the Immigration requirements applicable at the relevant port or entry point.

Applicants or travellers may be required to provide:

1. Valid passport or travel document.
2. Evidence of the purpose of entry.
3. Other documents required by Immigration.
4. Any applicable fee.

The exact eligibility and conditions depend on the circumstances of the traveller.

The current official Immigration guidance should be consulted for the exact Landing Pass requirements.
