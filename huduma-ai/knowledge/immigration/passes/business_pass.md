# Business Pass

A Business Pass is intended for foreigners entering Tanzania for short-term business-related activities.

It may be appropriate for temporary activities such as:

1. Auditing.
2. Conducting feasibility studies.
3. Establishing business contacts.
4. Making arrangements for investments during the start-up period of a company.
5. Fixing or repairing machinery.
6. Short-term professional assignments.
7. Other short-term lawful business-related activities recognized by Tanzanian law and approved by the issuing authority.

The Business Pass should not be treated as a substitute for a Residence Permit or Work Permit when the intended activity requires longer-term employment or residence.

General supporting documents may include:

1. Valid passport copy.
2. Covering letter from the relevant company or institution.
3. Evidence explaining the business activity or assignment.
4. Previous pass where applicable.
5. Prescribed fee.

The exact requirements depend on the nature of the business activity and the applicant's circumstances.

Current official pass information should be checked before payment because fees and requirements may change.
