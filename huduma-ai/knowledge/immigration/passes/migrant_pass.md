# Migrant Pass

A Migrant Pass is an Immigration pass category for eligible persons under the applicable Tanzanian immigration framework.

The official Immigration Services Department lists Migrant Pass among the pass information categories.

Applicants should provide the documents required for their specific Migrant Pass application.

General supporting documents may include:

1. Valid passport copy.
2. Application or covering letter.
3. Supporting evidence for the applicant's status or purpose.
4. Previous pass where applicable.
5. Prescribed fee.

The current official Immigration pass guidance should be used to determine eligibility, required documents and the applicable validity period.

The current official fee schedule lists a Migrant Pass fee of TZS 10,000.
