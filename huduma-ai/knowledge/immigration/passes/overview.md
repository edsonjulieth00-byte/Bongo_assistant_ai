# Tanzania Immigration Passes — Overview

An Entry Pass is permission issued to a foreigner to enter or remain in Tanzania for a specified purpose, subject to the Immigration Regulations.

The current Tanzania Immigration e-Immigration Pass portal lists these main pass services:

1. Business Pass
2. Dependant Pass 1
3. Dependant Pass 2
4. Exemption Certificate
5. Special Pass
6. Student Pass 1
7. Student Pass 2

The Immigration Services Department also provides information on other pass categories, including Migrant Pass, Visitor Pass, Landing Pass, Re-entry Pass and In Transit Pass.

A pass is different from a Residence Permit. A pass is normally used for the specific entry or temporary purpose defined for that pass.

General documents commonly required for pass applications include:

1. A valid passport copy.
2. A covering letter from the relevant institution, employer or organization where applicable.
3. The previous pass for renewal where applicable.
4. The prescribed fee where applicable.
5. Additional documents required for the particular pass category.

The maximum validity of a pass should not exceed two years.

Applicants should use the official Tanzania Immigration e-Immigration Pass portal and the current official Pass Guidelines for the exact category requirements and applicable fee.
