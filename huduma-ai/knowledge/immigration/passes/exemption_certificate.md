# Exemption Certificate

An Exemption Certificate is an Immigration document issued to persons who qualify for exemption from particular immigration requirements under applicable Tanzanian law.

The official e-Immigration Pass portal lists Exemption Certificate among its available pass services.

Eligibility depends on the applicant's legal status and the applicable exemption.

Supporting documents depend on the basis of the exemption and may include:

1. Valid passport or travel document.
2. Official letter or documentation establishing the applicant's status.
3. Supporting documents from the relevant institution or authority.
4. Other documents requested by Immigration.

Applicants should not assume that employment, study or residence is automatically exempt from immigration requirements. The relevant legal exemption must apply.

The current official Immigration Guidelines should be checked for the exact eligibility, procedure and documentation.
