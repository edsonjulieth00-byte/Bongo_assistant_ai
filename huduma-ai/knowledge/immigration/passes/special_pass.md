# Special Pass

A Special Pass is an Immigration pass used for specific temporary circumstances authorized by the Immigration Services Department.

The official e-Immigration Pass system lists Special Pass as an available pass service.

The applicant must provide the documents relevant to the specific purpose for which the Special Pass is requested.

General supporting documents may include:

1. Valid passport copy.
2. Application or covering letter explaining the purpose.
3. Supporting documents from the relevant institution, employer or organization.
4. Previous Immigration document where applicable.
5. Prescribed fee where applicable.

A Special Pass should not automatically be treated as a substitute for a Work Permit, Visa or Residence Permit. The applicable Immigration authority determines the appropriate status.

Applicants should use the current official Special Pass Guidelines for the exact eligibility and supporting documents.
