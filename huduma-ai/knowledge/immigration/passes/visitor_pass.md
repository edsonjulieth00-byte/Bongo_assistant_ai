# Visitor Pass

A Visitor Pass is an Immigration permission associated with a visitor entering or remaining in Tanzania for an eligible visit.

The visitor must satisfy the applicable entry and immigration conditions.

Supporting documents depend on the purpose and circumstances of the visit and may include:

1. Valid passport or travel document.
2. Evidence of the intended visit.
3. Return or onward travel information where required.
4. Other documents requested by Immigration.

A Visitor Pass should not be used to conduct activities that require a Work Permit, Residence Permit or another specific immigration status.

Applicants should confirm the current official Immigration requirements before travel or application.
