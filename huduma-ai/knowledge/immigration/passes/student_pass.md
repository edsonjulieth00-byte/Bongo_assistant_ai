# Student Pass

A Student Pass is for foreign students and other eligible persons entering Tanzania for approved academic or educational purposes.

Student Pass categories include Student Pass 1 and Student Pass 2.

A student or prospective student should have documentation from the recognized educational institution hosting or admitting the applicant.

Supporting documents may include:

1. Valid passport copy.
2. Admission or introductory letter from the host institution.
3. Evidence of the course or programme and its duration.
4. Evidence of sponsorship or scholarship where applicable.
5. Previous Student Pass for renewal where applicable.
6. Prescribed fee or evidence of exemption where applicable.

Student Pass requirements can differ according to the applicant's education programme, institution and nationality.

Applicants should use the current official Immigration Pass Guidelines to determine the correct Student Pass category and current fee.
