# In Transit Pass

An In Transit Pass is associated with a foreigner passing through Tanzania in transit to another destination.

Transit should be genuine transit to another country and the traveller must comply with the conditions imposed by Immigration.

Supporting evidence may include:

1. Valid passport or travel document.
2. Evidence of onward travel.
3. Visa or permission for the destination country where applicable.
4. Other documents requested by Immigration.

The traveller should not use an In Transit Pass for activities that require another immigration status.

The exact requirements and conditions should be confirmed from the current official Immigration guidance.
