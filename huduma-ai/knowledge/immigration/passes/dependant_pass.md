# Dependant Pass

A Dependant Pass is intended for an eligible dependant of a person who has the right to reside or remain in Tanzania.

The official e-Immigration system provides Dependant Pass 1 and Dependant Pass 2.

Supporting documents depend on the relationship and status of the person on whom the dependant relies.

They may include:

1. Valid passport copy of the dependant.
2. Covering or supporting letter.
3. Evidence of the relationship, such as a marriage certificate or birth certificate where applicable.
4. Copy of the principal person's passport and relevant Immigration document.
5. Previous dependant pass for renewal where applicable.
6. Prescribed fee where applicable.

The exact category, eligibility and fee should be confirmed against the current Immigration Pass Guidelines before application.
